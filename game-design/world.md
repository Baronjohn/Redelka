# World

## Setting

**Decided:** The game takes place in a **fully fictional world** — not historical Europe, though the aesthetic may evoke late gothic / pre-industrial isolation.

**TBD:**

- World name and map geography
- How many regions exist beyond the chapter locations
- Calendar, technology level, and social structure

## Tone and mood

**Decided:** **Folk horror** is the primary flavor.

- Isolated communities holding to old rites
- Pagan or pre-Christian beliefs treated as real and dangerous
- Nature and superstition as threats equal to monsters
- Tragedy and guilt tied to the land, not cosmic indifference alone (though deeper mysteries may lurk)

Surface tone: gothic, mournful, uncanny. The player should feel like an intruder in places that were never meant to be disturbed.

## Protagonist

**Decided:** **Single fixed protagonist** — the story orbits one lead character.

**TBD:**

## Playable cast

**Decided:**

- **5–7 total playable characters** recruited across chapters
- **Party size in battle: 1–4**, set by story moment or player choice where allowed
- **Medium-choice roster:** story forces some lineups; otherwise the player picks who fights

**TBD:**

- Full cast list, roles (melee, ranged, support, etc.)
- Which chapters lock party composition vs. allow free selection
- Per-character **skills** (see [progression.md](progression.md)); global **spell** pool for magic
- Optional party banter triggers and rest-point conversations

## Antagonists and factions

**Decided:** Enemies are **small elite groups** (1–4 per fight), not mobs. Boss fights may occupy multiple tiles on the 5×5 grid.

**TBD:**

- Primary antagonist or force (cult, entity, curse, etc.)

## Key locations / chapters

**Decided:** **Chapter-based structure** — 8–10 distinct self-contained locations sequenced by the story. No single open-world hub required.
