# Redelka — Overview

## Vision

**Redelka** is a modern reimagining of the design DNA behind *Koudelka* (1999): a folk-horror tactical RPG where fixed-camera 3D exploration and claustrophobic grid combat carry equal weight. The player leads a single protagonist through a fictional gothic world, scavenging scarce resources, uncovering old rites, and surviving elite encounters on a shared 5×5 battlefield.

This is **loose inspiration**, not a remake. Story, characters, and setting are original; the mechanical and tonal pillars echo the classic.

## Design pillars

1. **Even split: horror exploration and tactical combat** — Neither pillar is subordinate. Dread, puzzles, and avoidance matter as much as positioning and turn economy on the grid.
2. **Resource pressure over power fantasy** — Melee weapons break, ranged weapons consume ammo, gear is scavenge-only and often rare. The player is never fully comfortable.
3. **Agility-driven tempo** — Turn order is not fixed rounds; a CTB timeline means faster characters act more often, and movement range is a per-character identity stat.
4. **Visible threat, scripted dread** — Enemies exist in the world and can be avoided; ambushes and fixed encounters deliver horror beats without relying on random encounters alone.
5. **Player agency within constraints** — Hybrid stat growth, party selection from a medium cast, dialogue choices, and tactical options (wait, retreat, move/action ordering) — but no facing chess, no shops, no free saves on Hard.

## Genre balance

| Pillar | Role |
|--------|------|
| Folk horror atmosphere | Isolation, old gods, pagan rites, environmental dread |
| Fixed-camera exploration | Authored framing, tracking cameras, chapter-based locations |
| Tactical grid combat | Shared 5×5, CTB, move + one action, elite enemy design |
| Survival economy | Durability, ammo, scavenge-only loot, post-battle injury treatment |

## Target platform

- Godot 4.7+
- GDScript
- 3D with fixed (tracking) cameras in exploration; tactical grid view in combat

## Scope

| Dimension | Target |
|-----------|--------|
| Playtime | 12–20 hours |
| Chapters | 8–10 distinct locations |
| Playable cast | 5–7 characters; party size 1–4 per battle (story-dependent) |
| Economy | Scavenge-only; no currency or shops |
