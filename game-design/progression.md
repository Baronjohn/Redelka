# Progression

## Stat growth

**Decided:** **Hybrid allocation** on level-up.

- **Automatic base growth** per character (preserves class/archetype identity)
- **Bonus points** the player assigns freely each level (steers build without full manual allocation)

**TBD:**

- Stat list (e.g. Strength, Agility, Vitality, Luck — names and count)
- Points per level and automatic curves per character

## Magic and skills

**Decided:**

- Magic/skills are **unlocked** by **story progress** or **defeating specific foes** — not bought or found as generic scrolls alone.
- Once unlocked, a skill earns **XP through use** in battle.
- Skills can **level a capped number of times**, unlocking **stronger tiers** of the same spell (e.g. tier 1 → tier 2 → tier 3).
- Characters may have **unique spells or abilities** in addition to shared unlocks.

## Roster and party selection

**Decided:**

| Dimension | Rule |
|-----------|------|
| Total cast | 5–7 playable characters |
| Battle party | 1–4 members |
| Selection | Story locks composition in key moments; otherwise player chooses lineup |

**TBD:**

## Progression ↔ equipment

- Power also comes from **rare gear** (fixed drops, secrets, farming) — see [equipment-economy.md](equipment-economy.md).
- Gear is **scavenge-only**; no shops. Progression must not assume buying upgrades.

## Progression ↔ difficulty

- Save rules and optional **sanity system on Hard** may affect effective progression pressure — see [difficulty-saves.md](difficulty-saves.md).
